---
description: Frame prompts as decisions rather than pre-answered outcomes, for the rest of this session
---

Use the `neutral-prompt` skill and apply it to every prompt you write, review, or
rewrite for the rest of this session: separate settled constraints from open
decisions, name every admissible outcome, replace protected actions with
observable criteria, keep the intensity symmetric across outcomes, and ask for
the decision record rather than a particular conclusion. These rules persist
until I say "stop neutral mode" or "normal mode".
